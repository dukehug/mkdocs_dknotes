## 1. The Actor

### 1.1 **Cybercrime  vs  Cyberterrorism**

| Cybercrime                            | Cyberterrorism                                       |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Usually motivated by financial gain   | Motivated by ideological or terrorist objectives     |
| Focuses on profit, theft , or fraud   | Focuses on fear, disruption, or intimidation         |
| Targets individuals and organizations | May target critical infrastructure or public systems |

An Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) is a prolonged and targeted cyber  
campaign in which an attacker maintains access to a specific network or  
organization for an extended period.  

### 1.2 **The three important words are:**  

**Advanced:** The attacker may have sophisticated skills, tools, and  
resources.  

**Persistent:** The attacker attempts to maintain access over a long period.  
Threat: The attacker intentionally targets a system or organization

**Threat:**  The attacker intentionally targets a system or organization 

### 1.3 Typical APT characteristics 

- Carefully selected targets
- Long-term operations
- Stealthy behavior
- Intelligence gathering
- Multiple stages
- Persistent access
- Often associated with well-funded groups 

**Key idea:** An APT is not simply a single attack. It is usually a long-term, targeted campaign. 


## 2. Attack Lifecycle

The attack lifecycle describes the different stages an attacker may go through when attempting to compromise a target.  

### 2.1 **Cyber Kill Chain** is the commonly used model.

| Stage                   | Main Activity          | Example                                       |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Reconnaissance       | Gather information     | Research employees                            |
| 2. Weaponization        | Prepare attack         | Prepare malicious payload                     |
| 3. Delivery             | Send attack            | Phishing email                                |
| 4. Exploitation         | Abuse weakness         | Exploit vulnerable software                   |
| 5. Installation         | Establish access       | Install malware                               |
| 6. Command and Control  | Maintain communication | Compromised system communicates with attacker |
| 7. Actions on Objective | Achieve goal           | Steal or disrupt information                  |


## 3. Comparing the Threat Actors


| Threat Actor        | Main Motivation                                  | Typical Skill /Resources |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Cybercriminals      | Financial gain                                   | Low to highly organized  |
| Insiders            | Revenge, profit, mistake, or compromised account | Depends on access        |
| Hacktivists         | Political / social cause                         | Low to high              |
| Nation-State Actors | National / geopolitical interests                | Very high                |
| Script Kiddies      | Curiosity, entertainment, attention              | Usually low              |
| Cyberterrorists     | Fear, disruption, ideological objectives         | Varies                   |
| APT Groups          | Long-term intelligence or strategic objectives   | Usually highly capable   |
