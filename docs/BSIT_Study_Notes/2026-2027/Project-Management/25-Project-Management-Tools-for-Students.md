## Learning Goals

After reading this note, students should be able to:

1. Explain four main types of project management tools.
2. Compare free plans, paid plans, strengths, and limits.
3. Choose a suitable tool for a student project.

## Tool Categories

Some tools can fit more than one category. The table below shows their main use.

| Category | Main purpose | Tools in this note |
|---|---|---|
| General and team collaboration | Share tasks, files, messages, and team updates | Trello, Asana, ClickUp, Notion, Microsoft Planner, Google Workspace, Slack, Basecamp |
| Agile and Scrum | Manage a backlog, sprints, Scrum boards, Kanban flow, and issues | Jira, monday.com, Wrike, Zoho Projects, KanbanFlow, Taiga, Scrumwise |
| Mind mapping, planning, and visualization | Show ideas, diagrams, plans, and structured data | Miro, MindMeister, Lucidchart, Xmind, Airtable |
| Academic-friendly and lightweight | Start quickly, work with a small team, or make a simple plan | GanttProject, Quire, Freedcamp, Taskworld, nTask |

### Cost Words Used in This Note

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Free plan | You can keep using a limited version without paying. |
| Paid plan | You pay for more users, storage, views, security, or other features. |
| Free trial | You can test paid features for a short time. It is not a permanent free plan. |
| Included | The tool has no extra cost only when you already have a related subscription. |
| Open source | The software code is available. Self-hosting can be free, but setup and server costs may still exist. |

> Pricing and plan limits can change. The information below was checked on 2026-08-26. Tool names link to official plan pages.

## 1. General and Team Collaboration Tools

| Tool | Free or paid? | Main strengths | Main limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Trello](https://trello.com/en/pricing) | Free plan for up to 10 people and 10 boards in one Workspace. Paid plans are available. | Very easy Kanban boards; fast setup; good for clear task ownership. | The free plan has limited boards, file size, automation, and project views. It is weak for complex dependencies. |
| [Asana](https://asana.com/pricing) | Free Personal plan for up to 2 users. Paid plans are available. | Clear list, board, and calendar views; good task structure; many integrations. | The free team size is very small. Timeline, Gantt, forms, custom fields, and stronger reporting need a paid plan. |
| [ClickUp](https://clickup.com/pricing) | Free Forever plan. Paid plans are available. | Many tools in one place, including tasks, Docs, boards, calendar, and basic sprints. | The large number of features can confuse new users. The free plan has only 60 MB of file storage and limits some advanced features. |
| [Notion](https://www.notion.com/pricing) | Free for personal use. Paid plans are available. Eligible university students and teachers may get Plus at no cost. | Combines notes, wiki pages, databases, and project tasks; very flexible. | Teams on the free plan have limits. Setup can take time, and it is less strict than a dedicated project management tool. |
| [Microsoft Planner](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/planner/microsoft-planner-plans-and-pricing) | Basic Planner is included in eligible Microsoft 365 plans. Premium Planner plans cost extra. | Works well with Teams and other Microsoft 365 apps; simple board, grid, schedule, and chart views. | It is not a separate permanent free service. Advanced project features need a premium license. |
| [Google Workspace for Education](https://edu.google.com/workspace-for-education/editions/education-fundamentals/) | Education Fundamentals has no cost for qualified schools. Business editions are paid. Personal Google apps also have free versions. | Excellent real-time work in Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Meet, and Calendar; familiar to many students. | It is a group of apps, not one full project tracker. Teams must design their own task process, often in Sheets or Tasks. |
| [Slack](https://slack.com/pricing) | Free plan. Paid plans are available. | Strong team chat, channels, file sharing, notifications, and app connections. | The free plan gives access to only the most recent 90 days of messages, and older content can be deleted. It does not replace a full task manager. |
| [Basecamp](https://basecamp.com/pricing) | Free personal plan for 1 project and up to 5 users. Paid plans are available. Approved class work can be free for schools and universities. | Simple all-in-one space for tasks, messages, files, chat, and schedules; fixed-price team plans. | The normal free plan is very small. It has fewer advanced workflows and reports than Jira, ClickUp, or Asana. |

## 2. Agile and Scrum Tools

| Tool | Free or paid? | Main strengths | Main limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Jira](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/jira/pricing) | Free for up to 10 users. Paid plans are available. | Strong Scrum and Kanban support; backlog, sprint, issue, report, and workflow tools. | It can be difficult for beginners. The free plan has 2 GB storage, community support, and limited automation. |
| [monday.com](https://monday.com/pricing) | Free forever for up to 2 seats and 3 boards. Paid plans are available. | Colorful and flexible boards; many templates; easy status tracking. | The free plan is too small for many student teams. Scrum work often needs custom setup, and advanced views need payment. |
| [Wrike](https://www.wrike.com/price/) | Free basic plan. Paid plans are available. | Good task and project structure; board and table views; useful for formal team work. | Gantt charts, dashboards, and many planning features need a paid plan. The interface may feel heavy for a small class task. |
| [Zoho Projects](https://www.zoho.com/projects/zohoprojects-pricing.html) | Free for up to 5 users and 3 projects. Paid plans are available. | Free Gantt view, subtasks, whiteboard, mobile apps, and Google/Microsoft connections. | Time tracking, advanced dependencies, budgeting, custom fields, and stronger reports need higher plans. |
| [KanbanFlow](https://app.kanbanflow.com/pricing) | Free plan with no time limit. Premium is paid. | Clean Kanban board; built-in Pomodoro timer and time tracking; easy WIP limits. | Gantt, attachments, advanced reports, revision history, and many integrations are Premium features. It is less complete than Jira. |
| [Taiga](https://community.taiga.io/t/paid-plans-are-coming-back-to-taiga/8534) | Free open-source self-hosting. Taiga Cloud has a free plan with 1 public project, 1 private project, and 10 MB storage; paid cloud plans are available. | Made for Agile teams; supports Scrum, Kanban, epics, issues, and a backlog; no per-user cloud fee. | The free cloud storage is very small. Self-hosting needs technical setup and server maintenance. |
| [Scrumwise](https://www.scrumwise.com/pricing.html) | No permanent free plan. It has a 30-day free trial, then a paid per-user plan. | Focused Scrum design; simple backlog, release, sprint, task board, burndown, Kanban, and time tools. | Students must pay after the trial. It is narrower than an all-in-one collaboration suite. |

| Tool | Free or paid? | Main strengths | Main limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Miro](https://miro.com/pricing/) | Free plan with 3 editable boards. Paid plans are available. Accredited schools can apply for a free Education plan. | Excellent for brainstorming, workshops, diagrams, sticky notes, and visual planning; many templates. | On the normal free plan, only 3 boards stay editable and boards are not private inside the team. Large boards can become messy. |
| [MindMeister](https://www.mindmeister.com/us/pricing) | Free Basic plan with 3 mind maps. Paid plans are available. | Easy online mind maps; unlimited collaborators on the free plan; useful presentation mode. | Only 3 maps are free. | [Lucidchart](https://lucid.co/lucidchart/trial) | Free plan with 3 editable documents and 60 shapes per document. Paid plans are available. | Strong flowcharts, process maps, system diagrams, and professional templates. | The free limits are tight. It is better for diagrams than for daily task management. |
| [Xmind](https://xmind.com/pricing) | Free plan with core mind-mapping tools and up to 10 online maps. Paid plans are available. | Clean mind maps, many structures, presentation tools, and useful exports on paid plans. | Real-time collaboration, editable Gantt charts, larger online limits, and advanced exports need higher plans. |
| [Airtable](https://airtable.com/pricing) | Free plan for small teams. It allows up to 5 editors and 1,000 records per base. Paid plans are available. | Combines a spreadsheet with a database; flexible views, forms, links, and simple automations. | It takes time to design a good base. Free records, automation, API calls, and advanced views are limited. |

| Tool | Free or paid? | Main strengths | Main limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| [GanttProject](https://www.ganttproject.biz/about/payment-paddle) | Free and open-source desktop software. A paid download is optional support for the project. | Works offline; good Gantt charts, dependencies, milestones, and resource planning; no account needed. | Desktop-first design has weak real-time collaboration. | [Quire](https://quire.io/pricing) | Free for up to 10 members and 4 projects. | [Freedcamp](https://freedcamp.com/pricing) | Free plan with unlimited tasks, projects, and total storage. Gantt, recurring tasks, custom fields, and several advanced tools need payment. |
| [Taskworld](https://taskworld.com/pricing/) | A 14-day Business trial is clear. The same official page also mentions a limited free version, but its current user limits are not shown consistently. ## Recommendations for Students

| Student need | First choice | Why | Cost note |
|---|---|---|---|
| A very simple class project | Trello | Easy to learn and easy to see task status. | Free for a team of up to 10 people. |
| Notes, research, and tasks in one place | Notion | Pages, tables, files, and tasks can stay together. | Check the free student Plus offer. |
| Documents and group writing | Google Workspace for Education | Best for live work in Docs, Sheets, and Slides. | Often provided by the school at no cost. |
| A small software Scrum project | Jira | Strong backlog, sprint, board, and report tools. | Free for up to 10 users. |
| A simpler Kanban project | KanbanFlow | Easy board plus timer and WIP limits. | Useful permanent free plan. |
| Open-source Agile work | Taiga | Good Scrum and Kanban features with self-hosting choice. | Free cloud is small; self-hosting needs technical skill. |
| Brainstorming and workshops | Miro | Fast visual work with many templates. | Normal free plan has 3 editable boards; Education plan may be free. |
| Formal diagrams and flowcharts | Lucidchart | Makes clear, professional diagrams. | Free plan is enough only for small diagrams. |
| An offline Gantt chart | GanttProject | Free desktop planning with dependencies and milestones. | Fully usable without a subscription. |
| Many features in one free tool | ClickUp | Tasks, Docs, boards, calendar, and sprints are together. | Free storage is very small. |

### A Simple Choice Rule

1. Start with the project method: simple tasks, Scrum, visual ideas, or Gantt planning.
2. Count the team members.
3. Check whether the free plan includes the views and storage you need.
4. Use a school license or education offer before paying.
5. Test one small project first. Do not move the whole team until the tool feels easy.

## Related Class Topic: Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe 6.0)

SAFe is not a project management app. It is a framework for using Lean and Agile practices across many teams in a large organization. SAFe 6.0 was released as a major update to SAFe 5.1.

![SAFe 6.0 Big Picture](https://img.dukehsu.com/study_note/20260826001407749.webp)

### Six Main Themes in SAFe 6.0

1. **Stronger business agility:** Connect strategy, value streams, leadership, and team work.
2. **Clearer team responsibilities:** Explain the work of Agile Teams, Product Owners, Scrum Masters or Team Coaches, and other roles.
3. **Faster value flow:** Use eight flow accelerators, WIP limits, Kanban, and value stream management to reduce delays.
4. **Agility across the business:** Apply Lean and Agile ideas outside IT, such as in finance, marketing, HR, legal, and operations.
5. **AI, Big Data, and Cloud:** Give guidance for using these technologies in products and value streams.
6. **Better outcomes:** Use Measure and Grow and optional OKRs to connect work with measurable results.

### SAFe Studio

SAFe Studio is Scaled Agile's online platform for learning and using SAFe. It includes courses, role-based learning, assessments, planning tools, practice resources, certification management, and a professional community.

Access normally comes from one of these paths:

- joining an approved SAFe course;
- buying individual Studio access; or
- joining an organization with a SAFe Enterprise subscription.
