## 1. Project Execution Definition 


Project Execution - sometimes called project implementation, is the process of putting activities outlined in your project plans into action. 

This is usually the longest phase in a larger process called the project management life cycle. 


## 2.Project Execution Element

- Project scope
	- illustrate the roles and responsibilities of individual team members and highlight important elements that may affect the end result of the project. 
- Standards for quality and technical aspects 
	- 
- Goal  - Target 
- Resource - What you have 
- Schedule and time line - Time management 
- Organization - Monitoring 
	- Key personnel details 
	- Decision-making roles
	- Methods
	- Approach toward team involvement

## 4. Common challenges during project execution 

**Scope Creep**

- New features, tasks, timeline, resources, or budget, leading to delays and cost overruns. 


**Poor communication**

- A lack of clear channels or updates between teams, leadership and  clients causes misunderstandings , duplicated effort and missed milestones.


**Inadequate Resource Management**

- Team members become overbooked, underutilized, or lack the right tools and skills to complete assigned work efficiently. 


**Delays in Decision-Making**

- Slow sign-offs or bureaucratic bottlenecks stall progress and create cascading schedule conflicts

**Budget Overruns**

- Actual spending outpaces estimates due to rising costs, unapproved changes, or poor burn-rate tracking.
