## 1. Define 

DEFINE is where we turn research insights into a focused problem frame.

### 1.1 What Does Define Mean

- Focus
	- clear problem space
	- observations
- Meaning
	- find underlying needs
	- not only surface complaints
- Frame
	- create a problem frame that guides later ideation
- Share
	- build a shared understanding the whole team can use


## 2. Analysis & Synthesis

### 2.1 Analysis

- Break information into parts
- sort, label, and categorize notes.
- identify patterns and themes.

### 2.2 Synthesis

-  Combine parts into new meaning 
- Form insights and opportunities
- Prepare for a clear problem frame




## 3. Good Problem Statement

Research 

### 3.1 Characteristics of a Good Problem Statement 

- Human-centered - Focuses on people and their needs, not on a technology or solution
- Broad enough - Leaves room for many creative solutions, does not prescribe one answer
- Narrow enough - Specific enough to give the team clear direction and focus
- Actionable - Written so the team can start generating ideas immediately
- Insightful. - Grounded in real research insights, not assumptions or opinions. 
- Clear & concise - Easy to understand and share with others in a short statement 


### 3.2  Define  Problem Statement Tools 

#### a. Affinity Diagrams

- Saturate and Group notes into themes

Saturate and Group process:

1. Put all notes on a wall (Saturate)  
2. Cluster similar notes together  
3. Label each cluster with a theme  
4. Highlight priority themes. 

!!! example  "Result"
	 Clear themes from messy data



#### b. Empathy Mapping

- Capture what user say, think, do feel

- Says
- Thinks
- Does
- Feels

!!! example  "Result"
	 Deeper understanding of the user


#### c. Point of View

- User + Need + Insight statement 

Formula: 

> [User] need [Need] , Because [Insight]


!!! example  "Example"
	 An e-commerce company wants to track package records because it wants to improve its after-sales service.


#### d. How Might We

- Turn problems into opportunity questions

Question list:  

- How might we help the company track its packages more effectively?
- How might we reduce the number of lost packages?
- How might we help the company improve its package tracking process?



#### e. Why-How Laddering



Move between abstract needs(WHY) and concrete solutions (HOW) to find the right level for your problem 

- Move between abstract needs & concrete ideas

WHY - Abstract Need

MID - Problem Focus

HOW - Concrete Ideas
