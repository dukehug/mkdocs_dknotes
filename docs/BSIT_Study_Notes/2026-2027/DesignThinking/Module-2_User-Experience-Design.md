### 1.0 User Experience Design Definition 

- It is about the user experience
- In IT industry ,  user experience (UX) refers to the overall experience users have while interacting with a system.
- The satisfaction of the user's needs from the system

**My own opinion** 
- UX has a direct impact on user retention.
- UX is an important metric for evaluating business goals and product success.
- As designers, we should think from the user's perspective while also considering business needs.

### 2.0 Factors that Influence User Experience

#### 2.1 User Experience Honeycomb


![user_experience_honeycomb](https://img.dukehsu.com/blackboard/user_experience_honeycomb.webp)

Useful 
- Solutions should be creative and innovative to fulfill the need

Usable 
- Solutions should be easy to use

Desirable
- Use of emotional design to make the solution attractive and enticing

Findable
- Solutions should be navigational in which information in the solution can be easily found

Accessible
- Solutions should be designed also for people with disabilities

Credible 
- Provided information and executed processes by the solution is true and believable

Valuable
- Solution must provide benefits that are beyond the expectations of the users
